## How to use it

- Keep your lab and assignment work here from the start, not only at submission time.
- Commit small and often, with messages that say what changed. Your commit history is the record of your work and is what the instructor looks at.
- Commit under your own GitHub account. Work pushed from someone else's account is not evidence of your contribution.
- Do not commit passwords, API keys, or files larger than a few megabytes.
- Do not share your code with other students or copy theirs. Your repository being private is what makes that easy to respect.
